## 📌 Executive Summary

- **Background:** B.Tech student in **Electrical & Computer Engineering** at **TKM College of Engineering, Kollam** (*Class of '28*), pursuing **Honours in Cybersecurity**, and fascinated by how data, models and hardware behave in the real world.
- **Core Philosophy:** Good systems are **measured, not assumed**. I rely on **quantitative evaluation**, **ablation studies**, and **fairness metrics** to show that a model, schema or circuit actually works.
- **Current Drive:** Training **graph neural networks** for sign language recognition, auditing **bias in financial ML models**, and designing **hybrid SQL + NoSQL** architectures.

---

## 🎯 Current Engineering Focus

- **ML Research:** Summer research intern on **continuous Indian Sign Language recognition**, building skeleton-based models with **GNNs** and **contrastive learning** for zero-shot recognition.
- **Responsible AI:** Detecting **proxy discrimination** and benchmarking lending models against **disparate impact** and **equal opportunity** standards.
- **Data Systems:** Designing concurrency-safe booking with **ACID transactions**, **3NF schemas** and **MySQL + MongoDB** architectures.
- **Embedded Systems:** Real-time sensing and alerting on **STM32** with **I2C** and **UART**, plus analog power electronics.
- **Security:** Cryptography, network security fundamentals and secure system design as part of my Cybersecurity Honours.
